Every survey measures in its own band, and a light curve drawn from several of them has to be put on one scale first. These are the relations that do it - the ones the light curves here are built from, and the ones stdpipe uses when it augments a catalogue - each written out as the code applies it, with where it comes from.

Convert a magnitude
—
Magnitude to flux zero points from the SVO Filter Profile Service

A magnitude is a ratio and nothing more, and the zero point is what it is a ratio to - the flux of a star of zero magnitude in that band. On the Vega system that star is Vega, so the number differs from filter to filter; the AB system is defined as a flux to begin with, and is the same 3631 Jy everywhere. Choosing a filter sets the system its survey publishes on, which may then be changed. Either field may be typed in, and the other follows.
