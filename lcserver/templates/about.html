Model atmospheres

The archives above are what was observed. These are what it is compared with: grids of model spectra, computed and published by the groups named below, which the SED fit interpolates between to arrive at a temperature, a gravity and a reddening. A fitted parameter is a statement about a grid as much as about a star, and the grid is the part of it a reader cannot see - so each one is named here, and the model explorer shows what any of them actually says.
